# Why I Chose Chemical Engineering

## Introduction

Choosing a future field of study is not an easy decision. Many disciplines offer opportunities to solve problems and make a positive impact on society. After exploring different areas of science and technology, I became particularly interested in Chemical Engineering because it combines chemistry, mathematics, innovation, and real-world applications.

## Discovering My Interest in Chemistry

My interest in chemistry started with curiosity. I wanted to understand how substances interact, why materials have different properties, and how scientific principles can be used to create products that people use every day. As I learned more about chemistry, I realized that it is present in nearly every aspect of modern life, from medicine and energy to food production and manufacturing.

One area that especially captured my attention was perfume science. Learning about fragrance ingredients, distillation, and how perfumes interact with the skin showed me that chemistry is not only theoretical but also highly practical and creative.

## Why Chemical Engineering?

Chemical Engineering stood out to me because it goes beyond understanding chemical reactions. Chemical engineers apply scientific knowledge to design processes, improve efficiency, and solve large-scale problems. They transform laboratory concepts into products and technologies that benefit society.

I am attracted to the field because it requires both scientific knowledge and problem-solving skills. Chemical engineers work on challenges related to energy, materials, manufacturing, environmental protection, and many other industries that shape the modern world.

## Connection to Sustainability

Another reason I am interested in Chemical Engineering is its role in sustainability. Environmental issues such as pollution, waste management, and resource conservation are becoming increasingly important. Chemical engineers contribute to developing cleaner technologies, reducing industrial waste, improving recycling processes, and creating more sustainable products.

As someone who is interested in sustainability, I believe Chemical Engineering provides an opportunity to contribute to meaningful solutions that can improve both society and the environment.

## Future Goals

My goal is to continue learning about chemistry, engineering, sustainability, and perfume science while developing the skills needed for future academic and professional opportunities. Through this portfolio, I document my learning journey, projects, and interests as I explore the field of Chemical Engineering.

Although I am still at the beginning of this journey, I am excited to continue learning and discovering how science and engineering can be used to create positive change in the world.

## Conclusion

Chemical Engineering combines many of the subjects and interests that inspire me, including chemistry, innovation, sustainability, and problem-solving. For these reasons, it is the field I hope to pursue in the future, and I look forward to learning more about its applications and possibilities.
